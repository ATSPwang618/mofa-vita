The reference is itself a reconstruction of extNagano 1.2.4.726 and states
that it follows Kirikiri's original-license / GNU GPL dual licensing.
This Rust/GLES/WGSL adaptation replaces CPU image buffers with managed GPU
sampling and bounded tables. It intentionally approximates visual behavior;
it is not the original DLL, nor a claim of pixel-identical emulation.
